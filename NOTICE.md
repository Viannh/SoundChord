# SoundChord for macOS: Notices & Attributions

SoundChord for macOS is an independent native macOS music player created and maintained by **Viannh**, inspired by **BitChord** by Kushagra Singh ([`kushagrasinghx/BitChord`](https://github.com/kushagrasinghx/BitChord)), and distributed under the **GNU General Public License v3.0 (GPLv3)**. See [LICENSE](LICENSE) for full terms and [CREDITS.md](CREDITS.md) for detailed ecosystem credits.

## Creator & Maintainer
- **Viannh**: Creator and maintainer of SoundChord for macOS (`https://github.com/Viannh/SoundChord`).

## Upstream Project
- **BitChord**: Copyright (c) Kushagra Singh (`kushagrasinghx`) and contributors, licensed under GNU GPLv3.
  - Repository: https://github.com/kushagrasinghx/BitChord
  - Modifications: Native macOS 15 Swift/SwiftUI/AppKit/AVFoundation implementation targeting `x86_64` and Apple Silicon with a zero-dependency Rust DSP chord, beat-grid, and section analysis library (`RustDSP`), native macOS glass/themed UI, CoreAudio/AVAudioEngine playback, local SQLite/JSON storage, multi-platform URL playlist importer, and local Discord IPC Rich Presence.
